## Trivia

- The team first worked on a different game, "Vexx and the Mezmers", before scrapping it and starting over with a new story, settings and art. ([Time Extension](https://www.timeextension.com/features/the-making-of-secret-of-evermore-squares-western-secret-of-mana))
- The team started with Brian Fehdrau, Doug E. Smith and Alan Weiss and grew to about 25 people. ([Time Extension](https://www.timeextension.com/features/the-making-of-secret-of-evermore-squares-western-secret-of-mana))
- The ring menu was written from scratch because the Redmond team could not reuse code from Secret of Mana. ([Rice Digital](https://ricedigital.co.uk/the-story-behind-secret-of-evermore/))
- SAGE was a Mac application connected to SNES development hardware, so artists could walk a character across a map and check colors on a real NTSC TV. SIGIL was a scripting language that let non-programmers build cutscenes. ([GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/?p=69677))
- The score was the first game soundtrack by Jeremy Soule, later known for The Elder Scrolls. ([RPGFan](http://classic.rpgfan.com/features/Jeremy_Soule_Interview/index.html))
